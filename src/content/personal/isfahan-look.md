I built Isfahan for my own Obsidian vault, then separated the look from my notes and setup so it works in any vault.

## Four phases, set by the sun

Isfahan works out sunrise and sunset for your place, on your computer, and moves through four palettes in a day.

| Phase | When | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Dawn | the three hours after sunrise | warm paper and turquoise |
| Workday | from then until sunset | crisp lapis and turquoise |
| Evening | the two hours after sunset | deep indigo and gold |
| Night | from then until sunrise | midnight blue and moonlit turquoise, in low contrast |

Dawn and Workday are light; Evening and Night are dark, and Obsidian's light and dark mode switch with them. The status bar shows the current phase, and you can pick another by hand.

## What it changes

- **Tile motifs.** A tile band behind each note's title, a faint lattice in the file explorer, diamond checkboxes, and an arch over `[!mihrab]` callouts. Seven patterns to choose from, including Girih, Shamseh rosettes and Banna'i fret.
- **Fonts.** Titles and top headings in Cormorant Garamond, page text in Literata, both bundled with the plugin.
- **Page mode.** In Reading view a note reads like a printed page: footnotes move into the margin, the first section opens with a drop cap, and the sidebars fade while you read.
- **Property ribbon.** The Properties block becomes a single line in the phase's colors, in three styles.
- **The finish.** Callouts become raised cards, quotes sit in pressed-in panels, tables become cards with small-caps headers, and tags and inline code turn into chips.
- **A new tab.** An empty tab shows the time, the date and the phase, the weather for the next 12 hours, a search over the vault and your recent notes.

## Privacy

Sunrise and sunset are calculated locally, the fonts are built in, and there is no analytics of any kind. The only network use is the optional weather, from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), which needs no account or key.

## How it's built

Plain JavaScript and CSS, bundled with esbuild, with tests for the build, a privacy check, and loading and unloading the plugin. The screenshots on this page are generated by a script that renders a sample note in every phase. It runs on desktop Obsidian and is released under the MIT license.

## Install

Download `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` from the [latest release](https://github.com/saeed1262/isfahan-look/releases/latest) into `<your vault>/.obsidian/plugins/isfahan-look/`, then turn Isfahan on under **Settings → Community plugins**. It will also be available from Obsidian's community directory once it is listed there.
